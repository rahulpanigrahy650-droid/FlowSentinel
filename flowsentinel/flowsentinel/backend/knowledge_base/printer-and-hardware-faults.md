# Printer and Peripheral Hardware Faults

## Symptoms
Printer reports a paper jam with nothing stuck, prints blank pages, is offline, or a monitor, docking station or keyboard does not work.

## Printer paper jam or offline
1. Power the printer off, open every access panel and remove all paper including small scraps; check the rollers for torn pieces.
2. Fan the paper stack, make sure it matches the tray size setting and reload it flat.
3. If the jam message persists, clean the paper path sensors with a dry lint-free cloth and restart.
4. For "offline": check the network cable or Wi-Fi, ping the printer IP, and remove and re-add the printer queue on the print server.

## Docking station or monitor
1. Reseat all cables, try another port and cable, and update the dock firmware and graphics driver.
2. Test with a known good device to isolate the faulty part and raise a replacement request.

## Escalate if
The device is under warranty and needs a vendor engineer visit.
